## Context

Part Two consists of 24 rapid encounter dialogues (*kanbian* 勘辨) where Linji tests the realization of visiting masters, students, monastics, and officials.

The register here contrasts sharply with the formal sermons of Part One:
- Dialogue-heavy, terse, lively, and often comedic or martial.
- Frequent physical action: shouting (喝 *he* / *katsu*), slapping, grappling, seizing mats, overturning tables.
- Introduces famous Zen figures: the eccentric Puhua (普化), Elder Ding (定上座), Zhaozhou (趙州), Mayu (麻谷), and Deshan (德山).

---

## Stylistic & Register Decisions

1. **Shouts (喝)**:
   - Watson translated as "gave a shout"; Sasaki translates as "gave a shout" or "shouted".
   - In Persian: «استاد نهیبی زد» / «فریادی برآورد» / «بانگ برداشت» (consistent with the decision from Spec 002).
2. **Blows and Slaps (打 / 掌)**:
   - Direct, punchy verbs: «سیلی نواخت»، «چوبی حواله‌اش کرد»، «بر صورتش زد».
3. **Puhua's Eccentric Rhymes and Calls**:
   - Puhua's street calls (e.g. section 29: "Coming as brightness, hit brightness...") must preserve their rhythmic, incantatory character in Persian.
